# 11 · QA, visual review and proof

## What counts as evidence

A pass needs a specific acceptance ID, commit/build, environment, reproducible steps, expected/observed results and an artifact. Use logs for code assertions, raw samples for timing, real captures for rendering and recorded observations for human experience. Never paste invented outputs into a report. If the environment cannot perform a test, mark it UNTESTED or BLOCKED.

Automated browser checks and device emulation are useful but do not replace physical hardware. Playwright's WebKit is not branded Safari, and its Firefox integration uses a patched build; therefore real release-browser checks remain separate from the automated suite. [S21]

## Test pyramid

**Unit:** math/units, contact/slip helpers, drivetrain edge cases, scoring eligibility, chain banking, ordered checkpoint rules, unlock/reward idempotency, serialization/migrations, quality selection and lifecycle reducers.

**Integration:** physics fixtures, resets, track loading, asset lifetime, input normalization, pause-reason composition, event completion transactions, ghost playback, audio state and SDK callback ordering. Use fake SDKs only to exercise explicit success/error/duplicate/timeout paths.

**Browser:** real boot, valid driving input, a completed short fixture event, garage/settings, resize, iframe scroll behavior, storage denied, corrupted save, tab hide/return, context loss/recovery, absent WebGL2 and missing-asset recovery. Verify correct build-specific network requests and absence of development artifacts.

**Physical-device and human:** the reference matrix, gamepad disconnect/reconnect, mobile multi-touch, tablet overlays/safe areas, iOS sound interruption, sustained performance, visual polish and actual learning/handling feedback.

## Mandatory adversarial cases

Hold throttle while opening settings; hide the tab while an ad starts; close/reopen results after reward processing; return after a delayed SDK callback; fill storage; load an unsupported future save; interrupt a track download; lose the WebGL context during a drift; unplug the gamepad mid-corner; resize while touching two controls; start a new run while an old callback is pending. None may create free rewards, stuck throttle, duplicate game loops or destructive save resets.

Replay/photo mode must not advance events, progression, timers or ad opportunities as though it were active racing. A ghost recording must not introduce a collider. Asset unload must not dispose a texture still used by the garage or another chunk.

## Visual and experience rubric

Score each category 0–100 using a short written justification and linked observations, then calculate the weighted total.

| Category | Weight | What reviewers examine |
|---|---:|---|
| Driving feel | 25% | Initiation, sustained slide, transitions, recovery, car distinction |
| Visual craft | 25% | Car silhouette/materials, road/environment coherence, light, VFX clarity |
| Performance | 20% | Mandatory timing/load gates plus visible frame pacing |
| Usability/accessibility | 15% | First session, input methods, readability, recovery and settings |
| Content/replay value | 10% | Distinct routes, fair goals, progression, retry and garage |
| Reliability | 5% | Stability, saves, platform lifecycle and error behavior |

Internal release target: weighted score ≥88/100 and no category below 80. This rubric supplements, never overrides, objective mandatory gates. An unavailable test does not receive a guessed score. S2 and S3 review visual/perceptual evidence independently; the owner gives final subjective sign-off before publication.

## New-player test

Recruit at least five consenting real testers with a mix of input methods, including two touch sessions. Do not invent volunteers. Measure time from actual control to first valid drift, whether controls/chain scoring are understood, whether the first event is completed, and where frustration occurs. Target at least four of five achieving a valid drift within 60 seconds and completing a first event without verbal coaching. The small sample is directional feedback, not a population-level statistical claim.

Collect no unnecessary identity, sensitive data or raw personal recordings. Report anonymized observations. When access to testers is missing, ship the test script and mark the human gate pending; agents reviewing their own generated text are not five human playtesters.

## Competitor calibration

At G2, inspect three to five currently available browser drift/racing games through their actual playable builds where access permits. Record source/date, device, render setting and what was actually observed. Compare entry speed, handling, frame pacing, car materials, camera and route readability. Do not steal their assets or use scraped screenshots as our marketing.

Treat superiority as a target to evaluate, not a fact to proclaim. A small subjective comparison can guide polish but cannot establish “better than most games” across the entire market. Produce our own before/after captures to show real improvements.

## Bug severity

P0: harmful exposure, data destruction, inability to boot broadly or a major release integrity failure. P1: progression blocker, repeated crash, broken primary control/platform flow or a mandatory gate failure. P2: noticeable but recoverable defect. P3: minor polish issue. No known P0/P1/P2 defect may remain at OWNER_READY or the final release candidate. P3 limitations must be disclosed. A failed mandatory requirement blocks regardless of severity label. See `specs/14_PRE_OWNER_QA.md`.

## Evidence folder structure to create

Use `evidence/<commit>/<gate>/` with a report, raw data, logs, screenshots/video and checksums. Keep source-identifying metadata in reports, not secret account tokens in traces. Separate machine-generated logs from manual observations. Record reruns and failures rather than deleting them to present a misleading clean history.

The final matrix must distinguish tests passed in local mocks, local iframes, branded desktop browsers, physical mobile devices and authorized portal previews. None automatically implies the others.
