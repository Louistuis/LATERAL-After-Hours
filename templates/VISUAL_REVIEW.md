## Competitor reference panel

## Specific defects and fixes

For every deduction identify scene/frame, player impact, owner, severity and retest evidence. Check paint/reflections, scale, road seams, LOD transitions, shadows, smoke overdraw, UI legibility, camera collision, tire contact and audio transitions. Obscuring a weak scene with bloom or motion blur is not a fix.
